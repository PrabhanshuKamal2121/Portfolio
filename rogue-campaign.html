ROGUECAMPAIGN

AI Engineering FastAPI Multi-Agent Swarm XGBoost ML

RogueCampaign.ai is an autonomous, AI-driven email marketing platform designed to replace legacy WYSIWYG newsletter tools with a predictive, agentic workflow. We engineered an ecosystem that speaks the same language across semantic copywriting, HTML compilation, and machine-learning delivery optimization.

Role AI Engineer
Timeline 6 Months
Year 2026
Team Independent
(MY ROLE)

Designed the multi-agent swarm architecture utilizing Pinecone RAG for brand DNA adherence.

Developed the XGBoost Send Time Optimization (STO) engine to predict peak engagement hours.

Built the Telemetry Hub for dynamic A/B analysis of raw event streams.

Orchestrated the headless automation pipeline via n8n and FastAPI microservices.

Approach

01

System Architecture

We audited the limitations of current drag-and-drop editors to map the gaps in modern email workflows. The brief: engineer an autonomous backbone, don’t just build another WYSIWYG editor.

02

Agentic Evolution

A refined multi-agent system (Carl & Natt) that handles both semantic copywriting and rigid HTML table compilation. AI augmented workflows turned hours of manual formatting into milliseconds.

03

Telemetry & ML

Delivery timing treated as a mathematical certainty. Integrated XGBoost algorithms evaluating 365-day rolling engagement vectors to hit the exact local hour of peak probability.

04

Headless Automation

Omnichannel routing webhooks and FastAPI deployments functioning as one seamless pipeline, so a campaign moves from spreadsheet to enterprise SMTP without a single human touch.
