(THE INITIATIVE)

Say No to Drugs Campaign

One of the most vital outreach programs conducted by the NSS NSUT Cell focused on substance abuse awareness. The "Say No to Drugs" campaign was designed to educate students and local youth about the severe physical, mental, and societal impacts of drug addiction.

The initiative required comprehensive ground-level engagement. We began by designing clear, highly visible informational pamphlets that conveyed the urgency of the issue without being alarmist. Following the design phase, our student volunteers mobilized across the campus and surrounding areas to distribute the material directly into the hands of the youth, facilitating open conversations and spreading critical awareness.

(THE FOUNDATION)

About NSS NSUT Cell

The National Service Scheme (NSS) Cell at NSUT is a student-led initiative aimed at promoting social responsibility and community service. Part of a nation-wide program initiated by the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India, the cell conducts outreach programs, awareness campaigns, and volunteer efforts to foster societal development.

Core Objectives

Encourage Social Responsibility

Helping students get involved in community welfare and making a positive, tangible impact on society.

Develop Leadership

Providing opportunities for students to lead, organize, and execute large-scale social projects.

Raise Awareness

Organizing events to educate the public on critical issues like health, education, and environmental sustainability.

Support Local Communities

Assisting underprivileged populations by focusing on accessible education, basic healthcare, and overall well-being.

(ACTION)

Major & Impactful Events

Blood Donation Camps

Frequently organizing blood donation drives to save lives and support regional healthcare infrastructure.

Tree Plantation

Leading environmental conservation efforts with massive tree-planting events, waste management campaigns, and plastic reduction drives.

Health Awareness

Running programs focused on mental health, hygiene, and disease prevention for students and nearby communities.

Swachh Bharat Abhiyan

Participating in sweeping cleanliness drives across the campus and surrounding areas to promote hygiene and environmental care.

Educational Outreach

Conducting dedicated teaching programs for underprivileged children in nearby areas, focusing on basic education and essential skill-building.

Structure

The Organization

Programme Coordinator: Usually a faculty member at NSUT, responsible for overseeing all NSS activities at the institutional level.

Core Committee: Active student leaders who take charge of organizing, planning, and managing large-scale events.

Student Volunteers: The primary driving force. Students voluntarily participate to execute social service activities on the ground.
