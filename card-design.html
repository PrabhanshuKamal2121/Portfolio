BUSINESS CARDS

Brand Designer Photoshop Figma Identity

A brochure tells the story, but a business card seals the introduction. Built alongside the expo brochure for Fractal Overseas, I designed 6 premium business card variations for the executive team to hand out at the 6th Edition of World Trade Expo 2025.

Role Brand Designer
Timeline May 2025
Year 2025
Partner DEKGRAFIS
(MY ROLE)

Designed the core visual identity and typography layout using Figma.

Created 6 distinct layout variations tailored specifically for Abhinav Saxena.

Exported assets into Photoshop for high-fidelity 3D displacement mapping.

Adjusted dynamic lighting, rock texture overlays, and shadows to generate photorealistic production mockups.

Approach

01

The Goal

Create a physical networking asset for the World Trade Expo 2025 that felt as premium and heavily engineered as the digital systems Fractal Overseas builds.

02

Identity

Minimalist but bold. The layouts strip away the usual corporate clutter to focus entirely on the name, title, and contact vectors with precise typographic kerning.

03

Variations

Produced 6 distinct layout variations for Abhinav Saxena to allow for different print finishes (matte, gloss, embossed) while maintaining brand consistency.

04

Photorealism

Before going to the printers, I utilized advanced 3D displacement mapping in Photoshop to preview how light would interact with the card stock resting on natural rock textures.
