(CASE STUDY · 05)

BRAND BROCHURE

Art Direction Print Design Illustrator InDesign

Fractal Overseas was preparing for the 6th Edition of World Trade Expo 2025. They needed a physical anchor to secure B2B clients and establish immediate authority on the exhibition floor. I directed and designed this comprehensive 14-page brand concept brochure to bridge the gap between their technical operations and high-end visual storytelling.

Role Art Director & Designer
Timeline May 2025
Event World Trade Expo 2025
Client Fractal Overseas
(MY ROLE)

Established the visual aesthetic and thematic direction to resonate with enterprise-level B2B expo attendees.

Designed all 14 multi-page editorial layouts in Adobe InDesign, ensuring perfect grid alignment.

Executed custom vector graphics and image treatments in Adobe Illustrator.

Prepared all files for high-fidelity print production, including color profiles and packaging mockups.

Approach

01

The Objective

Stand out at the 6th Edition World Trade Expo 2025. The brochure needed to serve as a high-end portfolio piece that sales reps could hand to prospective partners.

02

Thematic Tone

Clean, authoritative, and minimalist. We removed corporate clutter and focused on large, striking product imagery paired with sharp typography to build instant trust.

03

Grid & Layout

Built on a strict 12-column print grid in InDesign to ensure that every margin, image bleed, and text block felt deliberate and highly professional.

04

Physical Packaging

The design extended beyond the pages. I created complementary mockups for Kraft paper bags, ensuring the entire physical takeaway felt like a premium brand experience.
